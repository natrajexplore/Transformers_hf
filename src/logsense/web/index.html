How labeling works
  • IPs, users, hostnames, MACs, emails and serials are replaced consistently (same real value → same fake value). It is pattern-based: read the lines before sharing them.
  • Pick the category you know is correct. Model predictions stay hidden until you evaluate, so they can't bias your labels.
  • Rules: successful logins, routine CoA/accounting and TRAFFIC denies are noise; GlobalProtect failures are vpn; a WLC uplink flap is link_event.
  • Lines that fit no category (posture, RADIUS server down, ISE alarms): press X to exclude them; the excluded count is reported.
  • Keys: 1–7 label · X exclude · ↑↓ or JK move · Backspace clear. Your draft is kept in this browser.
